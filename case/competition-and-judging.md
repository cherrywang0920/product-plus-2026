# Competition format and judging

Source: competition brief pasted by Cherry on Oct 18, 2026.

## Premise

SAP has "hired" our team to guide the strategy, vision and development of a new product from concept to launch and growth. Other teams were hired too; SAP picks the strongest idea backed by the best-developed plan.

## Product Arena (preliminary round)

- Submit **1-minute product pitch video** and **supporting PDF document**.
- SAP spends **only 5 minutes evaluating the whole submission**. The document must be easy to scan, with key information clearly organized and prioritized.
- Video should be creative, engaging and memorable.
- Video and PDF should **complement each other**, together communicating the product and the reasoning behind it.

### Scoring (out of 100)

| Criterion | What they look for | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Definition | A specific customer segment, user need and pain point; why this problem deserves priority | **25%** |
| Problem-Solution Fit | Features connected to user needs, priorities justified, how the experience improves | **25%** |
| Business Viability | Why retailers would adopt it, value to SAP, benefits vs costs, **assumptions made clear** | **25%** |
| Research & Supporting Evidence | Relevant, **traceable** sources; clear which claims are **verified facts vs assumptions** | **15%** |
| Video Pitch & Creativity | User, problem, solution and value proposition clear in one minute; purposeful storytelling, visuals, audio | **10%** |

## Product+ Summit (semifinals and finals)

Present to a panel of internal stakeholders with **different and sometimes competing priorities**. They test whether the product holds up across:
- **Software development** (technical feasibility)
- **Marketing** (go-to-market)
- **Finance** (development cost, value vs cost)
- **Overall product strategy**

Be ready to cover: technical feasibility, development cost, success metrics, go-to-market strategy, risks and constraints, and the **trade-offs** across product, development, marketing and finance.

**Prototype:** "build whatever conveys your vision best": interactive mockup, no-code prototype, or a functioning app.

### Scoring (out of 100)

| Criterion | What they look for | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Definition | Specific user, **current workflow**, evidence of pain points, reason to prioritize | **20%** |
| Product Solution | Feature priorities justified, improvement over alternatives, likely adoption, business value relative to cost | **20%** |
| Prototype | Core workflow shown through intuitive interactions and purposeful design; technical work counts when it meaningfully strengthens the solution | **20%** |
| Implementation Plan | Resources, dependencies, feasibility, risks, milestones, adoption, measurable success criteria | **10%** |
| Stakeholder Alignment | Address competing strategic, technical, financial and marketing needs; defend trade-offs with evidence; acknowledge limitations | **20%** |
| Presentation & Q&A | Persuasive 8-minute proposal, readable visuals, clear explanations, direct answers | **10%** |

## Rules

- You may not submit a project any team member worked on before Product+ began.
- Submit all deliverables by the deadline. Late or incomplete submissions may not be evaluated.
