Atelier, by Joule · Prototype hardware · Rev A

Atelier earpiece with boom mic

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan. Shown for the right ear: the tip points into the ear canal and the boom follows the cheek toward the mouth. Controls live on the ear unit: press the centre to talk to Joule, turn the ring for volume, and the top button is power. Green light means on, blue means Joule is active.
