# 60-second video: script and shot table

For the Product Arena submission (due Oct 20, 11:59 p.m.). A mix of **animated deck** scenes (hook, data, close) and **acted** scenes with motion-graphic overlays (problem and demo). Product name in the video: **Atelier, by Joule** (the PRD still says "Floor Signal" in places; pick one before export so the video and PDF match).

## What the video has to do (rubric check)

Video is 10% on its own, but judges watch it first, so it sets up the other 90%.

| Criterion | How the video covers it | Shots |
|---|---|---|
| Problem Definition (25%) | A dollar hook, then one user (atelier ops) and one runner in one moment (Saturday peak, room 3). The pain is shown: no alert, full hands, atelier leaves the rooms, client walks out | 1–4 |
| Problem-Solution Fit (25%) | Each demo beat answers a pain from Act 1: targeted alert → no alert; "Hey Joule" stock → wasted runs; voice substitute → lost nuance; haptic tap → "did anyone see it?" | 5–10 |
| Business Viability (25%) | Demand data → Business Data Cloud → dashboard; sold as a Joule extension on BTP; runs on existing earpieces | 11–13 |
| Research & Evidence (15%) | Hook number shown **with its working and sources, labelled as an estimate**; interview quote with a source line | 1, 3 |
| Video & Creativity (10%) | One story and one moment, not a feature list; the deck scenes use the PDF's style | All |

**One-sentence test:** if a judge remembers one thing, it should be: *"The request reaches the right runner by voice, and every request becomes demand data SAP never had."*

## The hook number

> "Did you know Aritzia could be losing close to **$100 million a year** on clothes clients ask for but never get?"

| Input | Value | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aritzia boutique (retail) revenue, FY26 | $2.41B CAD (65% of $3.70B) | S17, Aritzia FY26 results | Verified (primary) |
| Share of retail sales lost to out-of-stocks | ~4.1% (inventory distortion 6.2% of sales × 65.6% from out-of-stocks) | S4, IHL Group 2026 | Secondary: **confirm on IHL page before export** |
| **Estimate** | $2.41B × 4.1% ≈ **$98M a year** | Our calculation | **Assumption**: applies an industry-wide rate to Aritzia |

Cross-check: HBR's older ~4% stock-out lost-sales figure (S10) gives $96M, about the same.

**Wording rules:** say "could be losing" and "close to $100 million", never "is losing". Show the footnote on screen. Don't say this loss is *caused by* the hand-off; the hand-off is where we can catch some of it, and record the rest as demand.

**Backup hook** if IHL can't be confirmed: "1 in 2 shoppers leave a store without everything they came for" (Zebra Global Shopper Study, S6: 52%).

## Animation notes

Keep overlays and deck slides in the PDF's style (same colours, font and icons) so the two feel like one submission.

- **Deck scenes (1, 11–13):** build them as slides in Canva/Keynote/Figma with simple animations (counter, fade, slide), then screen-record or export as video. Use the same template as the PDF.
- **Counter (shot 1):** the number should land and hold for at least 1.5 s so the footnote can be read. Make the footnote small but legible at 1080p.
- **Waveform travel (shot 6):** the key visual in the acted part. Draw a line from Dora's ear to Brady's; dim everyone else. This shows "targeted, not broadcast" in one image.
- **Cards (shots 6, 9):** small rounded cards in the bottom third, short ease-in. Max 6 words each.
- **Store map (shot 7):** a flat 3-zone sketch (floor, back, other stores) is enough; it's on screen for 3 seconds.
- **Data tags (shot 11):** this is the business-viability beat; slow it slightly so the tags are readable.
- **Tools:** CapCut or Canva for cards and text; After Effects / Premiere if someone already knows it. Joule voice: any TTS (macOS `say`, CapCut TTS).
- **Captions:** burn in subtitles for all VO and dialogue. Many judges watch muted.

## Production plan (aim: 1 hour shooting, 1 hour deck, 2 hours editing)

Split the work so it runs in parallel:

| Who | Task |
|---|---|
| Dora + Brady + Cherry | Shoot acted shots 2–10 together (all three are on camera) |
| One person after the shoot | Build deck scenes 1, 11–13 in the PDF template |
| One person after the shoot | Record VO, rough-cut acted footage to it |

1. Record the VO first (one clean take of all lines) so the edit can be timed to it.
2. Shoot acted scenes in order 2–10; 2–3 takes each. Shoot the phone-screen close-up (shot 3) separately.
3. Film shots 4 and 5 from the **same camera position** so the rewind cut lines up.
4. Hold every shot 1 second longer than needed at each end for trimming.
5. Rough cut to the VO → drop in deck scenes → overlays → captions → music → export 1080p, ≤ 60 s.
