# Interviews

One file per interview: `YYYY-MM-DD-role-initials.md` (e.g. `2026-10-19-store-associate-jk.md`). Don't use full names or employer details the person didn't agree to share. Synthesis goes in `synthesis.md`.

## Template

```markdown
# <Role> at <type of retailer>

- Date / interviewer:
- Role, tenure, store or team size:
- Hypotheses tested: H1 / H2 / H3

## Their workflow
<!-- Step by step: what happens when demand changes. Who notices, who decides, what tool, how long. -->

## Last time demand surprised them
<!-- The specific story. Dates, product, what they did, what happened. -->

## Pain points (with quotes)
- "..."

## Workarounds and tools
<!-- Spreadsheets, group chats, emails, SAP screens, etc. -->

## What would change their behaviour

## Surprises / things that contradict our hypotheses
```
