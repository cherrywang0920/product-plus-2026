# Interview synthesis

Updated Oct 19. **Sample so far:** 3 store associates at one retailer (Aritzia), all friends of the team. That's enough to form hypotheses, not prove them.

| Theme | Evidence | Strength |
|---|---|---|

## What it means for our hypotheses

**H2 (store teams notice demand first but can't pass it on): supported, and more specific than we thought.**
Every retrieve request is a live demand signal: a client in the store asking for a specific item, size and colour *right now*. Today that signal:
- arrives late even inside the store (no notifications), and
- often isn't recorded at all, because staff skip CMT at peak, which is exactly when demand is highest.

**New insight to test: unmet demand is invisible.** When a client asks for a size that isn't in stock, no sale happens, so **sales data never shows that demand**. Forecasts and allocation run on sales data, so the business is blind to its own out-of-stocks in exactly the products that are trending. This could be our "gap between demand shifting and the business responding."
*Status: our inference. Not yet confirmed. Need to ask whether "couldn't find it" requests are logged anywhere and whether merchandising sees them.*

**H3 (alerts exist but get missed): reversed at store level.** Store staff have the opposite problem: no alerts at all. Dora's note captures the design tension: important updates must be pushed without flooding busy staff.

**H1 (merchants are slow because of weekly cycles): not tested.** We have no merchandising or planning voices yet.
